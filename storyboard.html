Story Arc

Before and Tension

Today, producer organizations manually upload geodata to cloud channels, wait for Fairtrade validation, then wait again for Satelligence risk analysis. With around 900 organizations in the pipeline, one organization can spend weeks or months in fragmented communication loops, with little automation and limited control for local members.

Shift and Outcome

A dedicated digital platform creates one orchestrated flow: guided geodata intake, quality checks, transparent validation stages, and faster analysis handoff. The result is shorter cycle times, clearer accountability, better producer experience, and stronger confidence in certification and EU compliance readiness.
Proof and Credibility

Signals that make this believable

Credibility comes from how the solution is built and governed: QA by design for data integrity at each step, security controls for sensitive location data, service design grounded in field-user realities, and robust technical implementation that scales across many organizations without losing auditability.
